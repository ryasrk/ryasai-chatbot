### 1. Purpose and Scope

1.1. This Customer Service Level Agreement ("SLA") defines performance standards, transit lead times, claim procedures, and liability limits for freight forwarding, warehousing, and transportation services provided by PT Arunika Logistik Nusantara ("Arunika").

1.2. Founded in 2011 with head office at Jl. Rungkut Industri III No. 18, Surabaya, Arunika employs 1,240 personnel (Dec 2025). This SLA governs operations across Arunika's five logistics hubs:
- **Surabaya Hub (SBY-01):** 18,000 m²
- **Jakarta Cikarang Hub (JKT-02):** 24,500 m²
- **Medan Hub (MDN-03):** 9,200 m²
- **Makassar Hub (MKS-04):** 7,800 m²
- **Balikpapan Hub (BPN-05):** 6,100 m²

1.3. Operations utilize Arunika's fleet of 312 trucks (140 CDD, 96 CDE, 52 Fuso, 24 Tronton) and 58 vans. Tracking and execution are managed via WMS "Gudangku v4", TMS "RuteKu", and ERP SAP Business One, overseen by CTO Hendra Gunawan and COO Yusuf Halim.

---

### 2. Service Windows and Warehouse Operations

2.1. **Commercial Window:** Administrative support, customer inquiries, and commercial billing desks operate Monday through Friday, 08:00 to 17:00 WIB.

2.2. **Warehouse Shifts:** Hub storage and cross-docking facilities run 24 hours daily across 3 operational shifts (Shift 1: 07:00–15:00 WIB, Shift 2: 15:00–23:00 WIB, Shift 3: 23:00–07:00 WIB). Personnel safety and staffing standards are maintained under CHRO Sekar Ayuningtyas.

2.3. **Dispatch Cut-Off:** Consignments processed after 16:00 WIB begin transit calculations at 08:00 WIB on the following business day.

3.1. Arunika commits to a contractual On-Time Delivery ("OTD") target of **97%** measured monthly per customer account across all contracted lanes.

### 4. Service Credit Framework

4.1. If aggregate monthly OTD falls below the 97% target, Arunika credits the customer on the subsequent monthly billing cycle issued via SAP Business One.

4.2. Service credits are calculated as a percentage of the customer's total monthly net freight billing in IDR, based on the penalty bands in Table 4.1:

#### Table 4.1: Service Credits per Missed OTD Band

| Achieved Monthly OTD | Missed Variance Band | Service Credit (% of Monthly Freight) | Action Required |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **≥ 97.0%** | 0.0% (Meets Target) | 0.0% (Nil) | Standard Monitoring |
| **95.0% – 96.9%** | 0.1% – 2.0% Missed | 2.5% Invoice Deduction | Hub Operations Review |
| **92.0% – 94.9%** | 2.1% – 5.0% Missed | 5.0% Invoice Deduction | Route Dispatch Audit |
| **88.0% – 91.9%** | 5.1% – 9.0% Missed | 10.0% Invoice Deduction | Formal Remediation Notice |
| **< 88.0%** | > 9.0% Missed | 15.0% Invoice Deduction | Executive Review by COO Yusuf Halim |

4.3. Service credits are applied solely as invoice offsets and cannot be disbursed as cash refunds.

---

### 5. Claims Procedure and Liability Cap

5.1. **Claim Window:** Clients must submit claims for delay, loss, or damage within **7 calendar days** of the delivery timestamp in TMS "RuteKu" through Jira Service Management. Claims logged after 7 days are deemed waived and void.

5.2. **Liability Cap:** Arunika’s liability for unexcused delay, loss, or damage is strictly capped at **10 times (10x) the freight charge** of the consignment, up to a maximum of IDR 100,000,000 per shipment.

5.3. Arunika is not liable for indirect, punitive, or consequential losses, including loss of business profit or operational downtime.

---

### 6. Exclusions

6.1. Arunika is exempt from SLA penalties and lead time targets under the following events:
- **6.1.1. Force Majeure:** Natural disasters, severe flooding, earthquakes, volcanic eruptions, civil disturbances, or official sea ferry suspensions ordered by Indonesian port authorities (Syahbandar/ASDP).
- **6.1.2. Shipper Deficiencies:** Misdeclared cargo weights exceeding truck capacities, improper packaging breaching SOP guidelines, incomplete customs paperwork, or inaccurate destination addresses.
- **6.1.3. Consignee Delays:** Unavailability of receiving staff, gate wait times exceeding 2 hours, or delivery refusal during standard business hours (08:00–17:00 WIB).
- **6.1.4. Regulatory Actions:** Mandatory vehicular inspections, weighbridge delays, or cargo holds by POLRI, customs (Bea Cukai), or the Ministry of Transportation (Kemenhub).
