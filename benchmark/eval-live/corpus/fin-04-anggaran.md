### 1. Purpose and Scope
This Standard Operating Procedure defines the governance, timeline, evaluation benchmarks, and administrative controls for preparing the FY2026 Annual Operating Budget and Capital Expenditure Plan of PT Arunika Logistik Nusantara ("Arunika"). 

This procedure applies across all business units and facilities operated by the company's 1,240 employees, including the head office at Jl. Rungkut Industri III No. 18, Surabaya, five distribution centers (Surabaya SBY-01, Jakarta Cikarang JKT-02, Medan MDN-03, Makassar MKS-04, and Balikpapan BPN-05), and transport operations comprising 312 trucks and 58 vans.

### 2. Expenditure Classification Standards
To ensure uniform financial accounting in SAP Business One, budget owners must strictly classify proposed spending into Capital Expenditure (CAPEX) or Operational Expenditure (OPEX).

#### 2.1 Capital Expenditure (CAPEX)
An item or project must be budgeted under CAPEX when it satisfies both of the following cumulative criteria:
1. **Value Threshold:** Individual unit purchase cost or aggregated project acquisition cost exceeds **IDR 25,000,000** (exclusive of VAT).
2. **Economic Useful Life:** The asset provides an economic and useful life greater than **one (1) year** (12 months).

*Permissible CAPEX categories include:* Fleet additions or major overhauls extending vehicle lifespan (CDD, CDE, Fuso, Tronton), electric forklifts, heavy-duty warehouse racking systems, dock leveler upgrades at JKT-02 or SBY-01, on-premise server cluster additions, and enterprise core customizations in SAP Business One.

#### 2.2 Operational Expenditure (OPEX)
Operational Expenditure encompasses routine recurring costs incurred to maintain normal operations:
1. Any asset purchase, repair, or tool replacement with a total cost of **IDR 25,000,000 or below**, regardless of its useful life.
2. Direct freight execution expenses, vehicle consumables (fuel, lubricants, tires, and toll road disbursements tracked in RuteKu), 3-shift warehouse utility bills, routine maintenance for 312 trucks and 58 vans, annual SaaS licensing for Gudangku v4 and Jira Service Management, and employee compensation.

### 3. Contingency Allocation Rules
To absorb macroeconomic fluctuations, diesel price increases, and unforeseen logistics disruptions:
- Every operational cost center must integrate a standard **5.0% contingency reserve** calculated against its total controllable OPEX baseline.
- Contingency reserves remain locked at the corporate treasury level and are not released for direct departmental disbursement upon budget upload.
- Drawdowns against the 5.0% contingency fund require an official justification ticket logged via Jira Service Management and countersigned by Chief Financial Officer Dimas Prasetyo.

### 5. FY2026 Budgeting Timeline (September – December 2025)
All milestones observe head office business hours (Monday–Friday, 08:00–17:00 WIB).

- **01 September 2025:** Official release of the FY2026 Budgeting Directive, macroeconomic baselines, and standardized SAP Business One upload workbooks by the Finance Directorate.
- **22 September 2025:** Deadline for departmental submissions. Budget owners submit itemized OPEX and CAPEX projections via Jira Service Management.
- **06 October – 24 October 2025:** Bilateral budget defense hearings. The Finance Directorate and CFO Dimas Prasetyo review submissions with each Budget Owner.
- **10 November 2025:** Delivery of the Consolidated Preliminary FY2026 Budget to the Executive Committee (Ratna Wijayakusuma, Dimas Prasetyo, Yusuf Halim, Hendra Gunawan, Sekar Ayuningtyas).
- **01 December 2025:** Formal transmission of the finalized FY2026 Annual Budget Dossier to the Board of Directors.
- **18 December 2025:** Formal approval of the FY2026 Annual Budget by the Board of Directors; system-wide cost-control baseline frozen in SAP Business One.

### 6. Quarterly Reforecasting Cycle
To preserve operational responsiveness across shifting market dynamics, Arunika conducts quarterly rolling reforecasts during FY2026:
- **Q1 Reforecast (Closing 10 April 2026):** Incorporates audited Q1 actuals, diesel pricing trends, and Gudangku v4 throughput statistics across all five hubs.
- **Q2 Reforecast (Closing 10 July 2026):** Half-year structural review evaluating delivery fleet utilization, maintenance costs, and software license commitments.
- **Q3 Reforecast (Closing 09 October 2026):** Pre-peak realignment assessing Q4 e-commerce freight surges and adjusting year-end capital expenditures.

Any operational cost center registering a year-to-date actual variance exceeding ±5.0% against its approved baseline must file a formal variance correction plan to the CFO within five business days of quarter close.
