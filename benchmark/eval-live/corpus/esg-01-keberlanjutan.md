## SUSTAINABILITY AND ENVIRONMENTAL POLICY

**Document Code:** POL-OPS-ESG-2026-002  
**Version:** 2.0  
**Effective Date:** February 1, 2026  
**Owning Department:** Operations & QHSE Division  
**Approved By:** Ratna Wijayakusuma (Chief Executive Officer), Yusuf Halim (Chief Operating Officer), Dimas Prasetyo (Chief Financial Officer)

---

### 1. Purpose and Scope

1.1. This policy formalizes the environmental protection framework, carbon mitigation mandates, and resource conservation standards for PT Arunika Logistik Nusantara ("Arunika"). It governs all operational units across freight forwarding, contract warehousing, and last-mile delivery.

1.2. The directives set forth in this policy apply to all 1,240 employees, temporary personnel, and registered contractors operating across Arunika’s head office at Jl. Rungkut Industri III No. 18, Surabaya, and all five regional distribution facilities:
- Surabaya Central Distribution Hub (SBY-01, 18,000 m²)
- Jakarta Cikarang Distribution Center (JKT-02, 24,500 m²)
- Medan Regional Logistics Hub (MDN-03, 9,200 m²)
- Makassar Gateway Hub (MKS-04, 7,800 m²)
- Balikpapan Logistics Terminal (BPN-05, 6,100 m²)

### 2. Carbon Emissions Baseline and Strategic Targets

2.1. In accordance with Arunika’s certified carbon audit for calendar year 2023, the organization established an operational emissions baseline across Scope 1 (fleet combustion, diesel backup generators), Scope 2 (grid electricity consumption), and Scope 3 (outsourced distribution legs and municipal disposal).

2.2. Arunika formally commits to reducing aggregate greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions by **30% (-30%) by December 31, 2030**, against the verified 2023 baseline.

### 3. Clean Fleet Operations and Transportation Controls

3.1. Arunika operates an active transport fleet comprising 312 trucks (140 CDD, 96 CDE, 52 Fuso, 24 Tronton) and 58 light commercial delivery vans. Fleet efficiency is administered in real time through the TMS "RuteKu" platform.

3.2. **Engine Idling Restriction:** A strict vehicle idling limit of **5 minutes** (300 seconds) is mandatory for all motorized equipment operating at warehouse loading docks, cross-docking staging lanes, and client delivery drop-off bays. TMS RuteKu has been configured to log telemetry exceptions automatically. Drivers who log more than 3 non-compliant idling events in a single calendar month must complete corrective eco-driving refresher sessions organized by Sekar Ayuningtyas (Chief Human Resources Officer).

3.3. **Jakarta EV Van Pilot Program:** 
- The company will inaugurate a pilot deployment of **12 electric vans** dedicated to last-mile urban logistics based out of the Jakarta Cikarang facility (JKT-02), commencing operations on April 1, 2026.
- A capital expenditure budget of IDR 7,450,000,000 has been allocated for the acquisition of 12 commercial electric delivery vans and four 60 kW dual-gun direct-current charging stations at JKT-02, recorded in SAP Business One under asset code `FA-EV-2026-JKT`.
- The EV van pilot is projected to displace 28,800 liters of diesel fuel and yield an annual net reduction of 76 tCO2e during the 2026–2027 fiscal cycle.

---

### 4. Warehouse Energy Management and On-Site Solar Power

4.1. **SBY-01 Solar Rooftop Installation:** Arunika has commissioned a **450 kWp** rooftop solar photovoltaic (PV) array at warehouse SBY-01 in Surabaya, utilizing 3,200 m² of unobstructed roof space.
- Total capital expenditure is IDR 4,680,000,000, financed under the approved 2025/2026 capital budget.
- The installation is projected to generate 635 MWh of clean electricity annually, offsetting approximately 490 tCO2e in Scope 2 grid emissions and supplying roughly 40% of daytime electrical demand for SBY-01.

4.2. **Continuous Shift Optimization:** Because Arunika’s five warehouses operate round-the-clock across 3 daily shifts, automated lighting controls and variable-frequency fan systems must interface directly with WMS "Gudangku v4". Operations supervisors are held accountable for maintaining low-power standby modes in unoccupied storage aisles.

---

### 5. Facility Waste Management and Pallet Circularity

5.1. **Waste Diversion Target:** Arunika establishes a minimum **waste recycling target of 60%** across all facility solid waste streams by December 31, 2026.

5.2. **Packaging Materials and Pallet Governance:**
- Stretch wrap (LDPE) and corrugated carton scrap must be compacted in designated balers located at SBY-01 and JKT-02 for collection by licensed recycling partners.
- Wooden and composite pallets tracked through WMS Gudangku v4 must achieve a minimum 80% refurbishment and reuse rate before retirement.

---

### 6. Compliance, Governance, and Reporting

6.1. CTO Hendra Gunawan and the Technology Division will ensure automated data feeds transmit fuel records from TMS RuteKu and facility utility meters into SAP Business One for carbon accounting. Operational deviations and hazardous spills must be reported through Jira Service Management within 2 hours under the `QHSE-ENV` project queue.

6.2. **Annual Reporting Cycle:** Arunika shall publish its comprehensive Annual Sustainability Report every calendar year during **Quarter 2 (Q2)**, detailing prior-year audit results, verified progress against the -30% by 2030 carbon target, and fleet electrification KPIs. Final approval must be countersigned by Yusuf Halim (COO) and Dimas Prasetyo (CFO) prior to public release.
